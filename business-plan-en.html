Industry Background
Organizational form is changing generations — this is not just an AI tool upgrade. In the future, the execution layer of a company will be entirely Agents; humans only manage.
§1.1

Organizational form is changing generations

For the past 200 years, every aspect of company design has been built to compensate for the limits of "humans as executors."

🧠

Human limits

Humans tire → shifts & rotations
Information bottlenecks → meetings & syncs
Specialty boundaries → department silos
Hiring cycles → headcount budgets
Turnover → knowledge management

🤖

Agent advantages

No fatigue · No information bottlenecks
No specialty boundaries · No hiring cycles
No turnover · Knowledge accumulates continuously

⚡

The trigger for restructuring

Last time: workshops → industrial corporations
This time: LLMs → AI-native organizations
Flatter · More dynamic · More elastic

The future company we want to see:

  • • Execution layer: specialized AI Agents, each taking on the function of an entire team
  • • Management layer: a small group of humans providing vision, key calls, external relations, and value judgments
  • • Interface layer: natural language. The boss states a goal; the organization decomposes it into agent-executable task chains
  • • Structure: no longer a pyramid, but a "task orchestration graph" that dynamically reorganizes around goals
New core problems

Human approval vs. Orchestrator authorization
SOP stability → autonomous iteration

Goal · Budget · Execution Model

From a single-sentence goal stated by the boss to executable task chains and budget consumption across an Agent cluster.

01
Goal Input
The boss states a goal in natural language; the organization parses it into an executable long-range goal plus constraints.
02
Budget Allocation
Tokens and operating budget are automatically allocated across the task chain, with overrun alerts and decision holds when budget is exhausted.
03
Execution Mode
CLI orchestration routes to different models; multi-Agent collaboration with cross-review; critical nodes escalate to HITL.
